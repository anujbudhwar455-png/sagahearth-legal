Sagahearth Privacy Policy

App: Sagahearth 1.2.0 (versionCode 9). Developer: Anuj Budhwar, trading as Desrein Studios, India. Effective date: 21 November 2026. Contact: Desreinstudios@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Sagahearth is an Android novel-writing app made by Anuj Budhwar, a solo developer, under the name Desrein Studios, in India (Asia/Kolkata). For questions, or to access, correct or delete personal data we hold, write to Desreinstudios@gmail.com.

We do not run a Sagahearth server that stores your novels. What you write stays on your phone unless you use a feature described below (AI help, Android backup, support email, or your account and its cloud copy).

2. Short summary

You use Sagahearth with an email-and-password account. Your projects, chapters, Story Bible, settings, covers and exports are stored on your device. When you are signed in, saved chapter text and short chapter memories may also be copied to a private space in our Firebase project, as described below.

AI features are bring-your-own-key: you paste your own Google Gemini API key in Settings, and when you use AI the text for that request goes from your phone straight to Google’s Gemini API with your key. We do not receive the key or the request. This version has no subscriptions, credit packs, ads, referrals or in-app payments, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. If Android backup is on, Android may back up your novels and some settings to your own Google account. Your Gemini API key is excluded.

3. Data stored on your device

This stays in the app’s private storage. We do not receive it unless a later section says so.

Your writing (the novels database on your device): projects, chapters, the Story Bible (characters, lore, plot threads, timeline, style, outline) and a local writing-quality phrase index.

Settings: theme, pen name or display name, writing goals and streak, High Fidelity and auto-rewrite choices, on-device style-match notes, onboarding, last opened project, and a local record that you are signed in (including your email).

Your Gemini API key(s) are stored only on the device in encrypted on-device preferences (EncryptedSharedPreferences, AES-256) and a master key in the Android Keystore. After you save a key it is not shown again and is not written to logs. In release builds, network logs are off and the key header is redacted. The key is never uploaded to us and is left out of Android backup and device transfer. You can add or delete keys in Settings. If the Keystore is unavailable, keys stay in memory only and are not saved in ordinary plaintext preferences.

Covers and images: a cover you pick is copied into the app’s private covers folder. Character or lore pictures are stored as a local photo-picker link. They are not uploaded to us. This version does not generate covers with AI.

Voice samples you import for a future voice-learning feature stay in the private voice_samples folder. In 1.2.0 they are stored and counted only, and are not sent to us or to Gemini.

Safety copies: the app writes a JSON snapshot of your library and a crash-recovery file in private storage so a damaged database can be rebuilt on the phone. You can also export or restore a JSON backup from Settings. Documents you import and chapters you paste for style match are read on the device; style-match measurements run on the phone.

The app’s only Android permission is INTERNET. It does not ask for contacts, location, SMS, or a persistent storage permission. Exports use the system file picker.

4. What leaves your device

4.1 Google Gemini (when you use AI)

The phone calls https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/ over HTTPS (generate content, and a simple key check). Your key is sent in the x-goog-api-key header, not in the URL. Cleartext traffic is disabled.

A request can include your prompt, the relevant chapter or a selected passage, Story Bible and Project DNA notes, outline beats, character or world details, and, for later books in a series, read-only canon from earlier books. The reply is saved on the phone if you keep it.

We do not operate that API and we do not see these requests or your key. Google processes the content under your key and under:

  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google APIs Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy

Unpaid (free-tier) quota. Google’s Gemini API Additional Terms (effective 23 March 2026) say that for unpaid services, including unpaid Gemini API quota, Google uses the content you submit and the generated responses to provide, improve and develop Google products and machine-learning technologies, consistent with Google’s Privacy Policy. Human reviewers may read, annotate and process API input and output. Google tells users not to submit sensitive, confidential or personal information to unpaid services.

Paid quota. Under the same terms, Google says it does not use paid-quota prompts or responses to improve its products, and it processes them under its paid-service data-processing terms. It may still log them for a limited time to detect policy violations and for required legal disclosures.

Which rule applies is between you and Google, and depends on the key and billing on your Google account. If you do not want story text sent to Google, do not use AI features and do not paste a key. Google’s terms also require API users (and API clients) to be 18 or older, and require paid Gemini services if the app is made available to users in the EEA, Switzerland or the United Kingdom. See our Terms of Use.

4.2 Android backup

If Android backup or a device transfer is on, Android may copy the novels database, on-device JSON safety snapshots, imported voice samples, and settings files to your Google account or new device.

Not backed up: your Gemini API key, Firebase sign-in tokens, and temporary crash-recovery preference files. Also not backed up today: cover files in the covers folder, and some Story Bible form-state preferences. Export a book file or JSON backup if you want your own copy of those. Backup is run by Google, in your Google account, not on a Sagahearth server. You can turn it off in device settings.

4.3 Support, reports, and what we do not do

If you email us (including from in-app support, or “Report output” when a report cannot be stored in the app), we receive your email address, your message, and any AI output or reason you include. We use this only to respond and review the report, and we keep it only as long as needed unless the law requires longer.

We do not show ads, include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, sell personal data, or use your writing to train our own models. This version has no in-app payments.

4.4 User accounts and sign-in

You use Sagahearth with an account. Firebase Authentication (Google) handles the account. We do not store your password. Email/password sign-in and password reset are processed by Firebase. When Google sign-in is shown, Google’s flow gives the app an ID token so Firebase can open or create your account, under Google’s Privacy Policy. We may store a display name or pen name you choose, and a local record that you are signed in (including your email in a settings file). Deleting an account does not, by itself, erase novels on the phone. See the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4.5 Cloud copy of chapters

If you are signed in, the app may copy to Cloud Firestore: saved chapter title, text, summary, project id and a timestamp, under a private space for your account; short chapter memories; and, if you use “Report output” while signed in, the reported text, your reason, your user id and a timestamp in a write-once reports collection (other users cannot read these reports in the app). Your Story Bible, covers, API key and most settings stay on the device unless you export them. Settings → Account → Delete account removes the Firestore copy and the Firebase account. Novels on the phone are kept; delete projects in the app or uninstall Sagahearth to remove local data. See the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. How long data is kept

On your device, until you delete a project, clear app data, or uninstall Sagahearth. Android backup copies last until you or Android delete them, or you turn backup off and they expire under Google’s rules. Support emails and emailed reports are kept only as long as needed to handle the request, unless a longer legal period applies.

Firebase accounts, Firestore copies and report records are kept until you delete the account, or we delete them after a request or when they are no longer needed (reports, only as long as needed to review them and meet Play or legal duties).

6. Security

We use HTTPS only, keep the Gemini key in EncryptedSharedPreferences, leave the key out of backups, and do not bake a key into the app. Your novels are in the app’s private storage. The novels database is not locked with a separate user password; it is ordinary app-private storage, not a vault. On a rooted or compromised device, a determined attacker may still read on-device files, including, with enough effort, a key that belongs to you. Treat your Gemini key like a password. You can revoke it in Google AI Studio or Google Cloud if you think it was exposed.

7. Children

Sagahearth is a novel-writing tool. The app itself does not ship mature content, and the Google Play content rating for this version is Everyone (3+).

Google’s Gemini API Additional Terms require API users to be 18 or older, and say the API must not be used in an app that is directed towards, or is likely to be accessed by, people under 18. If you use Sagahearth’s AI features, you must also follow Google’s Gemini terms, including that age rule. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 treats a person under 18 as a child. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data (for example by creating an account or emailing us), contact Desreinstudios@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, California’s CCPA/CPRA, or similar laws. These can include the right to know what personal data we have, to access or correct it, to delete it, to withdraw consent, to complain, and, where those laws apply, to request portability or to opt out of “sale” or certain “sharing.”

You can export your writing in the app (EPUB, DOCX, KDP PDF/DOCX, Story Bible Markdown and other formats, or a JSON library backup in Settings). You can delete local data by deleting a project, clearing the app’s storage, or uninstalling Sagahearth. Delete the account and cloud copy in Settings → Account → Delete account. Contact Desreinstudios@gmail.com about personal data we hold (for example a support email). We may need to confirm it is you.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. If a law gives you a right to opt out of “sale” or “sharing,” use the same address; we still do not sell that data. You may also complain to a data protection authority in your country, including, when applicable, the Data Protection Board of India, or a supervisory authority in your EU/UK country of residence.

On-device data stays on the phone unless you send it (AI features, backup, export, support email, or your account and its cloud copy). Google may process Gemini, Firebase and Android backup data in countries where Google or its partners operate, including outside India and outside the EEA. See the Google Privacy Policy.
