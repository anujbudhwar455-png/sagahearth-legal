Delete your Sagahearth account and data

This page explains how to delete your Sagahearth account and the cloud data tied to it. App: Sagahearth, by Desrein Studios (Anuj Budhwar), India. Contact: Desreinstudios@gmail.com.

How to delete your account in the app

Open Sagahearth → Settings → Account → Delete account.

This permanently removes your Firebase Authentication account and the cloud copy of your saved chapters and data stored in Google Cloud Firestore.

If you cannot use the in-app control

Email Desreinstudios@gmail.com from the email address on your Sagahearth account and ask us to delete the account. We will action the request within 30 days. We may need to confirm it is you.

What is deleted

Account deletion removes:

  • Your account, including the email address, display name, and user ID held in Firebase Authentication. We do not store your password.
  • The signed-in cloud copy of saved chapter titles, text and summaries, short chapter memories, and any “Report output” payloads stored in Cloud Firestore under your account.

What is kept

  • Novels and files stored locally on your own device are not deleted by account deletion. To remove those, delete the project in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall Sagahearth.
  • Content you sent to Google’s Gemini API under your own API key is governed by Google’s terms and privacy policy, not by this deletion. We do not receive that key or those requests, so we cannot delete them from Google. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service.

API keys stored on your device stay on the device until you delete them in Settings, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. They are never uploaded to us.
